Development and validation

Cohort297 patients, 16 hospitals in western Japan, hepatectomy 2000–2016; 60 futile resections
OutcomeRecurrence or death from any cause ≤6 months after R0/R1 hepatectomy (surgery-related deaths excluded)
DiscriminationAUC 0.78 apparent; 0.74 after bootstrap optimism correction; 0.71 (95% CI 0.63–0.78) in internal–external cross-validation by region
CalibrationBootstrap-corrected slope 0.82; Hosmer–Lemeshow P = 0.74
StatusExternal validation in an independent cohort is planned
